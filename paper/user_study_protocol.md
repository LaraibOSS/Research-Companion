# Research Companion — Pilot Usability Study Protocol

**Purpose:** provide the user-facing evaluation the EMNLP demo call asks
for, honestly framed as a small pilot. Runnable in one day with N = 3–5
participants (colleagues/lab-mates who write papers; note any who are
co-authors and exclude them from headline numbers).

## Design

Single-session, think-aloud, unmoderated-where-possible. Each participant
gets a laptop (or their own machine), the one-line install command, one
draft PDF, and one folder of 8–12 related PDFs (provide the same fixture
set to everyone for comparability — e.g., the repo's demo corpus). No other
instructions: the tool's onboarding is part of what is being tested.

## Tasks (in order, timed)

| # | Task | Success criterion | Measure |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | Install and open the Lab | Lab loads in browser | time from command to Home |
| T2 | Add the draft and ingest the folder | draft ★ set; ≥ 8 papers ingested | time; # of hesitations/wrong clicks (observer note) |
| T3 | "Is your bibliography covered?" | participant states N of M and what's missing, using banner/panel | time to correct answer |
| T4 | "Find one paper that challenges your Section X and show the evidence" | opens the alignment card, points at the verified quote | time; correct? |
| T5 | "Is this citation real?" (hand them one fabricated reference to check via review/refcheck) | states unverified + the reason | correct? |

## Questionnaire (after tasks; 1–5 Likert, 5 = strongly agree)

1. I could use the tool without reading a manual.
2. I understood what the tool had and had not verified.
3. The "Analysis covers N of M cited papers" disclaimer helped me trust the
   results appropriately.
4. I would use this on my own next submission.
5. (Open) What confused you most? What convinced you most?

Optionally append the standard SUS-10 if participants have time; the five
items above are the minimum reported set.

## Reporting (paper wording discipline)

Report: N, participant background (1 line), per-task completion rate and
median time, Likert medians, and 2–3 verbatim open-text quotes (with
permission). Frame explicitly: "a small pilot usability study (N = _)" —
no significance claims, no averages-of-averages. Negative observations are
reported, not filtered; that is consistent with the paper's honest-null
posture.

## Logistics for today

- 20–25 minutes per participant end-to-end.
- Observer sits behind, notes hesitations; no helping until a task is
  abandoned (mark abandoned, move on).
- Consent: one line — session observed and timed for a research paper's
  usability pilot; no personal data collected; quotes used anonymously
  with permission.
